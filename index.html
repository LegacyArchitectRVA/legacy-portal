Questions, answered

Before you buy.

Is this the same as a Life Manual?
No. This is the do-it-yourself starting point, covering the same seven pillars in a workbook you fill out on your own. A Life Manual is built with Craig over a set of sessions. This gives you a real first draft either way.
What format is it, and how do I get it?
It's a fillable PDF, 26 pages. After checkout it lands in your inbox automatically, no waiting, no account to set up.
Can I fill it out on my phone?
Yes. It's fillable on any device with a PDF reader, phone, tablet, or computer. Or print it and write it by hand if you'd rather.
Does anyone see what I write in it?
No. Nothing is uploaded or submitted anywhere. It stays on your device, and Legacy Architect RVA never sees your answers.
How long does it take to fill out?
There's no clock on it. Most people work through it a pillar at a time over a week or two, but you can do it in one sitting if you'd rather.
If I go on to build a full Life Manual later, does this carry over?
Yes. What you've documented here becomes the first draft, so you're not starting from a blank page when you build the real thing.
What if I get stuck on a question?
Leave it blank and keep moving. A blank field is information too, it shows you exactly where the gap is.
